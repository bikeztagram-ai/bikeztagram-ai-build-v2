# Bikeztagram AI — Long-Term Proprietary AI Roadmap

## Objective

Build Bikeztagram's own creative-generation technology progressively. Open/local models are treated as replaceable internal inference components, not the product itself.

## Phase 1 — Product-grade creative engines

Build the Bikeztagram-owned Creative Director, Music Engine, Video Engine, audio/event analysis, editing, QA, revision and export systems around the best practical open/local generation components.

## Phase 2 — Bikeztagram control and adaptation

Add stronger proprietary control layers: structured composition, section regeneration, subject continuity, scene planning, music-to-video co-direction, creative memory, ranking and domain-specific fine-tuning/adaptation where licensing permits.

## Phase 3 — Proprietary model components

Develop our own specialised models/components where they provide meaningful advantage: musical structure, event prediction, arrangement, continuation, stem operations, scene planning, subject consistency, motion/control and creative QA.

## Phase 4 — Proprietary foundation models

When compute, data, expertise and licensing make it viable, train Bikeztagram-owned foundation models for music and/or video using properly licensed, owned or otherwise lawfully usable training material.

## Data principles

- Prefer owned, commissioned, public-domain or clearly licensed training data.
- Record provenance and licence metadata.
- Do not build a training corpus by indiscriminately scraping copyrighted media.
- Keep model/data provenance auditable.

## Strategic rule

Do not delay the product waiting for a proprietary foundation model. Ship useful creative capability first, then replace underlying components progressively as our own technology becomes competitive.

## Success state

The user experiences one Bikeztagram AI creator. Under the hood, an increasing proportion of the creative intelligence and generative technology is Bikeztagram-owned, with external/open components remaining replaceable implementation details.
