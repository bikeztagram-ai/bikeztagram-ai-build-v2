# Model selection next steps

The benchmark harness is intentionally provider/model neutral.

## Music candidates
Evaluate open/local candidates for short-form composition, controllability, beat/section structure, licensing, VRAM and latency.

## Video candidates
Evaluate image-to-video and text-to-video candidates for subject consistency, motion, temporal stability, prompt fidelity, generation time, VRAM and licensing.

## Selection rule
Do not hard-code a model from marketing claims. Record actual benchmark evidence, verify commercial licence terms, then select the best practical local/open candidate per capability. Keep adapters replaceable.
