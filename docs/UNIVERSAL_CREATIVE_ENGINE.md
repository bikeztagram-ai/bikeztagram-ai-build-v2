# Universal Creative Engine

Bikeztagram is subject-agnostic. Motorcycle examples are benchmark cases, not product limits.

## Input
Natural-language idea, optional images/video/audio, optional subjects/references, duration, format and creative preferences.

## Decomposition
The Creative Director determines story, subjects, environment, actions, camera, style, audio, continuity and which assets should be real, transformed or generated.

## Generation
Use the best available eligible local/open model for each capability. Model adapters remain replaceable.

## Output
A coordinated sequence of real and generated media, original music/audio, captions where required, and requested export formats.

## Revision
Creative QA can request bounded regeneration or re-editing of weak sections.

## Safety and originality
Do not reproduce protected characters/assets or imitate living artists as a direct identity. Use high-level creative characteristics and original generated content.
