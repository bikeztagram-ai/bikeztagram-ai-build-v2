# Bikeztagram AI Music Studio — Target V1

## Quality target
Bikeztagram is not targeting background-loop quality. The long-term target is complete original songs with convincing songwriting, melody, arrangement, instrumentation, vocals where appropriate, dynamics, mixing/mastering and editable sections.

## Current candidate strategy
Benchmark strong local/open candidates such as ACE-Step 1.5 and YuE rather than locking the product to either model. Record exact model/version, runtime, hardware, licence and benchmark evidence before promotion.

## Bikeztagram-owned layers
1. Creative brief / songwriting direction
2. Composition and arrangement planning
3. Lyrics and vocal direction
4. Generation request compilation
5. Candidate generation
6. Quality ranking
7. Musical event extraction
8. Section/stem regeneration where supported
9. Mix/master processing
10. Music-to-video co-direction
11. Creative QA and revision

## Long-term path
Progress from open/local inference to Bikeztagram-specific adaptation and specialised proprietary components, then consider Bikeztagram-owned foundation music models when data, compute and licensing make that viable.

## Rights principle
Generated music must be original. Do not clone a living artist's distinctive voice or make output deceptively appear to be an artist's release. Training data must be owned, commissioned, public-domain or clearly licensed/otherwise lawfully usable with provenance recorded.
